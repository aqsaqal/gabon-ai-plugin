# Privacy notice for the GABON AI plugin

This notice describes what the GABON AI plugin for Claude Code does with data. It is not legal advice.

## What the plugin handles

Construction documents can contain personal data: names and positions of signers and contacts, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses and ИИН/БИН numbers. The plugin reads this data from the files in your project folder and writes it into the documents it produces in that folder.

## Where the data goes

- **The plugin's author receives nothing.** The plugin has no server, account, analytics or telemetry, and its scripts contain no code that sends your data anywhere.
- **Your files stay where you keep them.** Generated documents are saved only in the folder you choose.
- **Claude processes what it reads.** When Claude works with your documents, that content is handled by Claude Code and Anthropic under the terms and privacy policy of your Claude account.
- **Library downloads.** On first run, uv downloads Python libraries from PyPI. These requests fetch code and contain none of your documents. PyPI sees them as ordinary download requests.

## Retention

The plugin's author retains no data. Your files remain until you delete them. Uninstalling the plugin does not remove documents you created.

## Your responsibility

You decide which documents to process and with whom to share the results. If they contain other people's personal data, you are responsible for handling it lawfully, including under the personal data laws of the Republic of Kazakhstan.
